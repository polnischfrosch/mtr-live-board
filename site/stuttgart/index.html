Stuttgart Live Board

Test bench for the Stuttgart LED board (U1, U14, U2, bus 92 and 81), from the VVS departure monitor

Starting…

Two LEDs per stop, one per direction. Stadtbahn and buses run on the right, so each LED sits on the right of its direction of travel. A train or bus fades in as it leaves the stop before and is at full brightness at the platform. Stops load one by one (VVS limits requests), so the map fills in over about two minutes.
